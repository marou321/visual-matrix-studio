Transparency & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 · ChromaGlyph Studio is built to respect user privacy by keeping image processing strictly inside your browser while maintaining transparent disclosures for web advertising.

1. Client-Side Image Processing (Zero Server Uploads)

When you select or drag-and-drop an image file into ChromaGlyph Studio to generate Binary, ASCII, or Halftone artwork, that file is read exclusively within your local web browser via the standard HTML5 FileReader and Canvas 2D APIs.

  • We do not upload, transmit, inspect, log, or store your images on any remote server.
  • Generated PNG exports are encoded locally inside your browser and downloaded directly to your device.
  • Closing or refreshing the browser tab immediately releases your image data from local memory.

2. Browser Local Storage Preferences

ChromaGlyph Studio stores a single functional preference key (chromaglyph_theme) in your browser's localStorage solely to remember your preferred Dark or Light color mode between visits. This preference contains no personally identifiable information and is never shared with third parties.

3. Advertising & Google AdSense

To keep ChromaGlyph Studio free and accessible without paywalls or watermarks, this website displays advertisements served by Google AdSense (publisher account ca-pub-4588627708948117).

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies or similar device identifiers to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites across the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to ChromaGlyph Studio and/or other sites on the web.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.
  • To learn more about how Google manages data in its advertising products, please review Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

4. Contact Communications

If you contact us via email (hello@chromaglyph.studio) or our Contact page, we use your name, email address, and message strictly to respond to your inquiry or resolve your support request. We do not sell, rent, or trade contact email addresses to marketers or third-party data brokers.

5. Server Access Logs & Web Fonts

Standard web hosting infrastructure may automatically record routine non-sensitive HTTP request metadata (such as IP address, browser user agent, referring URL, and timestamp) strictly for security, abuse prevention, and uptime reliability. Our interface also loads web typography from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) to render legible monospace ASCII grids and editorial prose.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of personal data, as well as the right to opt out of personalized advertising cookies. You can manage browser cookies directly through your browser settings or Google's Ad Settings portal at any time, and contact us for any data inquiries.

7. Children's Privacy

ChromaGlyph Studio is a general-audience creative graphics application and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us via email, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements to the studio or changes in legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. For any questions about this policy, email hello@chromaglyph.studio or visit our Contact page.
