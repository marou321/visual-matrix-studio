Understanding the Settings

How to Adapt These Gallery Recipes to Your Own Photos

No two photographs share the same dynamic range or color gamut. Use the patterns below to translate any gallery recipe cleanly onto your own portraits, landscapes, or product shots.

Launch Studio →

Portraits & Rim Lighting

Start with Cyber Rim-Light Bitstream or LED Matrix Opacity Portrait. Lower the Binary threshold toward 95–110 when facial features sit in mid-shadow, and nudge saturation up to 125%–135% so subtle skin and rim colors survive glyph downsampling.

Landscapes & Soft Gradients

Skies, water reflections, and fog require smooth tonal stepping. Choose Aurora Chromatic ASCII or Tropical Petal Micro-Glyphs with a 9px or 8px grid and the Classic or 70-step Extended character ramp to avoid harsh banding across atmospheric transitions.

Graphic Architecture & Print

Hard shadows and geometric shapes shine in Offset Print Brutalist Study. Pair a light Gallery Paper backdrop with Size by Darkness Halftone dots and a +25% to +35% contrast boost for crisp editorial poster aesthetics.
