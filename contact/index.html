Reporting a Rendering Bug?

To help us reproduce issues quickly, please mention:

  • Your browser & OS (e.g., Chrome 130 on Windows 11 or Safari on iOS).
  • Active rendering mode (Binary, ASCII, or Dots) and approximate cell size.
  • Source image format and dimensions (PNG, JPG, WebP).

Privacy & Data Requests

Because ChromaGlyph processes uploaded artwork strictly on your device, we do not store user photographs. For questions regarding advertising cookies or GDPR/CCPA rights, please review our Privacy Policy or include "Privacy Inquiry" in your subject line.
